About me

I’m Jade.

My own fitness journey is a huge part of why I became a trainer. I know what it feels like to want to feel stronger, more confident and happier in your body, and I believe training should be about so much more than just how you look. It’s about looking after your body, seeing what it can do and appreciating everything it allows you to do.

Whether you’re new to the gym, looking to change your physique, training towards a challenge or simply wanting to feel your best, I want to help you find a way of training that you genuinely enjoy and that fits into your life.

Alongside personal training, I also teach Reformer Pilates and offer online coaching, combining training and nutrition support to help you stay consistent and keep progressing.

My goal is simple, to help you become the best version of you, whatever that looks like for you.
