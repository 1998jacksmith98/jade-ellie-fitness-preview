Privacy policy

How personal information is handled in connection with this website.

Who is responsible

Jade Ellie Fitness is responsible for this website. Reformer class bookings are made through Bookwhen. Enquire form submissions are sent by email via our form service. Those services act as separate processors for the data they handle.

What this site collects

If you use the enquire form, we collect the details you submit (such as your name, contact information, and what you’re looking for) so Jade can reply. If you email Jade directly, the information you send is used only to respond to your enquiry. Basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) may be processed by the hosting provider for security and performance.

Bookings

When you book a reformer class, you are taken to Bookwhen. Please review Bookwhen’s privacy information for how they handle your contact details and appointment data. Jade Ellie Fitness does not receive or store your card details.

Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality. It does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Jade Ellie Fitness. For bookings held by Bookwhen, contact them directly. To contact Jade about this policy: jadeelliefitness@gmail.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
